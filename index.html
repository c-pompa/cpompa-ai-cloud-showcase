cpompa-ai-cloud — architecture

IaC lab layout — VLAN 15, the network everything runs on

Every project on this page runs on hardware I own and segment myself. The lab was rebuilt in September 2026 onto a dedicated VLAN 15 (10.88.15.0/24, domain ai.internal) — the old flat 10.88.1.x network was decommissioned the same day. Click a node:

Why a dedicated VLAN

  • Blast-radius control. Prod (:8030) and dev (:8020) control planes, Postgres and Redis all live on the mini inside VLAN 15 — the rest of the home network can't reach the databases even by accident.
  • Tailscale overlay on top. GitLab (gitlab.cpompa.com), SSH and CI runners ride a WireGuard mesh (100.73.11.35) — CI deploys over the relay, never over an exposed port.
  • IaC-managed lifecycle. Services are declared in compose files, deployed by GitLab CI with SHA-pinned images (IMAGE_TAG=$CI_COMMIT_SHA), health-gated (--wait), with an automated prod rollback job.
